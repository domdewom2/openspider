## Empfehlungen und integration_handoffs

1. M2-U01/U10/N01: merge the optional detail region into U10 registerMainRegions alongside tabs/table; keep detail outside the upper-table main landmark. U01 owns placement and split-pane sizing. main.tsx registers DetailFrame separately. U10 publishes urlIds/focus/topTab/related/crawlId through setDetailSelection or DetailFrame.selection.

2. M2-U10/N01: inject actions.openBrowser=launchBrowser or consume cancelable os:detail-open-browser with detail { address }; call preventDefault when handled. Tauri never uses window.open.

3. M2-N01: replace feature-local read/merge/save queues by a shared zod-validated uistate updater for all regions (U10 tabs plus U11 tabs/columns); serialize the entire load/merge/save across features, ideally by atomic backend patch/revision checking across windows.

4. M2-K07/N01: emit the existing EventBus settingsChanged after crawl-config changes as well as app settings. DetailFrame now reloads config.get and cancels stale reads on this event.

5. M2-K06/planer/U10/N01: provide address-to-URL-id and Internal/External resolution scoped to crawlId, with a not-found result; view.locate only accepts ids. U10 must consume os:detail-navigate/os:detail-back until EventBus offers navigateAddress; then consolidate channels and preserve linkPath/source plus selection history.

6. M2-U21/N01: consume source navigation requests with stored linkPath; use view.update for same-schema sort/filter/search changes, adopt returned handle/version, clear stale row/selection caches and close obsolete handles. Keep view.open for schema/selection changes.

7. M2-K06: expose Hreflang Truncated counters in the hreflang detail-view response/metadata, with an aggregate for selected URLs, to replace one detail.urlDetails request per selected URL (currently batches of eight).

8. M2-N01: remove only the C9 fixme after K04/K06 merge and verify the real bridge flow; run ci.yml frontend/native matrix after publishing the reviewed fix.

Empfehlung 2 ist bereits clientseitig umgesetzt: `eventBus.on("settingsChanged")` erhöht eine Konfigurationsversion, `config.get` lädt neu und verwirft alte Antworten per AbortSignal. UI-033/UI-043 prüfen geänderten Rendering-/HTML-Speicherzustand und Abmeldung des Listeners. K07 muss Konfigurationsänderungen über diesen vorhandenen Vertrag melden; kein neuer erfundener Tick-Vertrag.

Empfehlungen 3–5 sind Entscheidungen für die genannten Besitzer: insbesondere bleibt die Adresse→ID-Auflösung bei K06, das gemeinsame View-Lifecycle-Update bei U21/N01 und die Hreflang-Zählererweiterung bei K06. U11 verändert deren eingefrorene DTOs/Commands nicht. Die obigen Handoffs sind die gebündelte Meldung an Planer/Integrator; es wurden keine Nachrichten oder Issues extern angelegt.
